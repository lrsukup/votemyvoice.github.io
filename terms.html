3 Description of Service

VoteMyVoice is a civic information tool that helps voters in Texas:

  • Check voter registration status via links or in-app lookups to official state systems.
  • View candidates and races appearing on their ballot based on their address or county.
  • Access publicly available campaign finance information sourced from the FEC and other public data providers.
  • View key election dates, early voting schedules, and polling place information.
  • Complete an issues quiz to explore alignment between their preferences and candidate positions.
