1 Who We Are

VoteMyVoice is a civic technology application designed to help U.S. voters check their voter registration status and find information about candidates running in their area. The current version serves voters in Texas, with an initial focus on Harris, Montgomery, Bexar, Dallas, and Tarrant Counties.

Websitewww.votemyvoice.app

2 Information We Collect

2a. Information You Provide

  • Your residential address — used to determine your county and jurisdiction so we can show you the correct ballot information.
  • Your name — optionally, if you use our voter registration status lookup feature.
  • Your account email and password — if you choose to create an account. Passwords are never stored in plaintext.
  • Feedback or support messages — if you contact us directly.

2b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Your IP address and general geographic region (city/state level — not precise location).
  • Device type, operating system, and app version.
  • Actions taken within the app (e.g., which county was searched).
  • Date and time of your session.

This data is used solely for security monitoring, debugging, and aggregate product analytics. It is not linked to your address input.

2c. Location Data (GPS)

VoteMyVoice may request access to your device's GPS location to pre-fill your address field for convenience. We do not store GPS coordinates. If you deny location permission, you can still use the app by typing your address manually.

3 How We Use Your Information

PurposeDetails
Voter registration lookupYour address or name is sent to state and county systems. It is not stored on our servers after the response is returned.
Ballot & candidate dataYour county is used to fetch relevant races from public data sources (FEC, Open States, Texas SOS). Only the county-level inference is used — not your full address.
Account servicesYour email is used to authenticate you and, if you consent, to send election deadline reminders. We do not use your email for marketing without explicit opt-in.
App performance & debuggingAggregate, anonymized usage data helps us fix bugs and improve the product. No personally identifiable information is included.
Legal complianceWe may use or disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4 Data Retention

Data TypeRetention
Address DataNot persisted. Used transiently to process your request, then discarded.
Account DataRetained while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion request.
Server LogsUp to 90 days for security and debugging purposes, then automatically deleted.
Analytics DataAggregate, non-identifiable data may be retained indefinitely for product improvement.
Device StorageQuiz answers and voter info are stored on your device only and never leave your device unless you are signed in.

5 Who We Share Data With

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with advertisers.

PartyWhat & Why
Texas Secretary of StateYour address/name is submitted to look up voter registration status.
FEC APICampaign finance data is fetched using jurisdiction parameters only. No personal data is sent.
Open States APIState candidate data is fetched using jurisdiction parameters only.
Google Civic Information APIMay be used to resolve your address to a voting district. Google's Privacy Policy applies.
SupabaseAccount credentials and quiz results (if signed in) are stored in a Supabase-hosted database bound to data protection standards.
Law enforcementOnly if required by a valid legal order, and only to the extent required.

6 Your Privacy Rights

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect; request deletion; opt out of the sale of your data (we do not sell it); and be free from discrimination for exercising your privacy rights. Contact privacy@votemyvoice.app to make a request.

Texas Residents

We extend the spirit of user control to all users. If you believe we have retained identifiable information about you and wish to request deletion, please contact us.

All Users

You may at any time delete your account from the app's Settings screen, or contact us to ask what information we may hold about you.

7 Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit and industry-standard authentication. Because we do not store your address data, the risk of a data breach exposing your voter information is substantially reduced by design.

To report a security issue: security@votemyvoice.app

8 Children's Privacy

VoteMyVoice is intended for individuals 18 or older (or 17-year-olds who will be 18 by the next election, as permitted for voter pre-registration in Texas). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us to report any concerns.

9 Third-Party Links and Services

VoteMyVoice may link to external websites such as county clerk offices, official election portals, and candidate pages. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites. When we transmit data to third-party APIs (see Section 5), those providers' privacy policies govern their use of that data.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as VoteMyVoice expands to new states and features. When we make material changes we will update the Effective Date above and notify users via in-app notice. Continued use of VoteMyVoice after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance.
